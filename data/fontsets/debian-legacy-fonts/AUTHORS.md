# Authors of the fonts in `data/fontsets/debian-legacy-fonts/`

Taken from the notices in the files and from Debian's `copyright.fonts`.

## misc-fixed variants and ETL fonts

Public domain, "donated (in unspecified order) by" H. Kagotani, BIZNET Poland, Inc., Dmitry
Yu. Bolkhovityanov, Thomas Bagli (the upper-half `c` glyphs of the 8x13 family, PCS Computer
Systeme, West Germany), Takahashi N. (ETL), Mark J. Reed, P. Peterlin, Mark Leisher, Roman
Czyborra, Kosta Kostis "and others". BIZNET Poland, Inc. made the ISO 8859-2 (`-IL2`)
versions; they are re-encoded to Unicode with the `trbdf` utility.

## Other fonts

- **Sony Corp.** (the 8x16 fonts), with BIZNET Poland, Inc. and Dmitry Yu. Bolkhovityanov.
- **/efont/ The Electronic Font Open Laboratory** (`h14`, `h16`, `h24` and their bold
  versions, and `georgian14`).
- **Roman Czyborra, David Starner and others** (GNU Unifont, an early snapshot).
- **Adobe Systems Incorporated** and **Digital Equipment Corporation** (Courier), with
  BIZNET Poland, Inc. and Dmitry Yu. Bolkhovityanov for the re-encodings.
- **Sun Microsystems, Inc.** and **Bigelow & Holmes** (Lucida Typewriter; Lucida is a
  trademark of Bigelow & Holmes, OPEN LOOK a trademark of AT&T), with BIZNET Poland, Inc. and
  Dmitry Yu. Bolkhovityanov for the re-encodings.
- **Gia Shervashidze** (`georgian16`).
- **Yitna Firdyiwek** of GohaTibeb Associates (the Goha and GohaClassic Ethiopic fonts).

## The `legacy*` fonts

Debian generated these automatically from the Linux console fonts of the `console-data`
package, so they carry the work of those fonts' authors. The documentation of `console-data`
credits, among others: Kosta Kostis (the `iso01` to `iso10` fonts, from `isofont101`),
H. Peter Anvin (the Latin-1 fonts), Pavel Zaboj (the Latin-2 fonts) with corrections by
Primoz Peterlin, Daniel Quinlan (`lat1-16`), Ricardas Cepas (the `lat4` fonts and
`LatArCyrHeb`), Sarantos Kapidakis and Angelos Karageorgiou (the Greek fonts), Bruno Haible
(`def2_8x16`), Andries Brouwer (`default8x16`, from his hardware), Raul D. Miller (`aply16`),
Ahmed Naas (the `16x.cp` fonts) and Daniel Yacob (the Ethiopic fonts). The authors of some of
the fonts used here (for example the Cyrillic, KOI8, `cp857` and `viscii10` ones) are not named
there.
